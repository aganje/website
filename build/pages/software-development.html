How We Work

Governed by default, not by review

We build with AI agents, inside the same governance we sell. That means the work arrives with its context, standards, and reasoning attached rather than reconstructed afterwards.

01

Your identity model

Access, roles, and data boundaries are your existing ones. Nothing is granted to an agent that a person would not be granted.

02

Your standards

Coding and architecture standards are applied to agent work as constraints, not as documentation nobody reads.

03

Traceable decisions

Each change can be traced back to the requirement, the context it was given, and the reasoning that produced it.

04

Human accountability

Technical leadership stays in the path. Agents propose; people approve and remain accountable.
